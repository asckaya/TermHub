**Solution**: Constructed an LLM multi-agent collaborative adversarial framework based on Stackelberg game. Proposed the CEEH (Complexity-Aware Entropy Regularization) RL optimization algorithm to dynamically evaluate reasoning difficulty and perform selective entropy regularization, preventing search space collapse in CoT trajectory optimization.

**Results**: Across 6 reasoning benchmarks, significantly reduced reasoning step lengths and token costs while maintaining or improving accuracy.
