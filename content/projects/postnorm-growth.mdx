**Solution**: Targeted feature boundary drift of Pre-norm during progressive depth expansion in LLMs. Explored the interaction between normalization placement and training curriculum, verifying the forward-conditioning advantages of Post-norm's self-normalized residual stream under block-stacking growth.

**Results**: Revealed the crossover phenomenon between normalization placement and training curriculum. In Qwen3-8B distillation experiments under curriculum growth, Post-norm reduced validation cross-entropy loss by 0.0328 over Pre-norm and maintained stable residual stream boundary scales.
