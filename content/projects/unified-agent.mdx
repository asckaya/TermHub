**Background & Solution**: Targeted slowness, high computational costs, and improper tool invocations of DeepSeek-V3.1 (671B) for DiDi's AI travel assistant "Xiao Di". Designed and developed a multi-role weight-sharing Agent RL framework based on Verl, constructing an Agent-as-Tool unified optimization strategy space for trajectory-level reward backpropagation.

**Results**: Achieved performance exceeding DeepSeek-V3.1 (671B) on complex tasks using GLM4-Flash (30B), reducing computational cost by 10x and significantly boosting inference speed.

## Tech Stack

- Verl (Volcano Engine Reinforcement Learning for LLMs)
- PyTorch, Transformers, Slime
- Multi-Agent RL & Strategy Optimization
- GLM4-Flash (30B), DeepSeek-V3.1 (671B)

## Highlights

- Powers DiDi AI travel assistant "Xiao Di"
- Shared-weight multi-agent RL framework
- Agent-as-Tool unified strategy space optimization
