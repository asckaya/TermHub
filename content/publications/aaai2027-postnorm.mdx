Pre-norm has become the default normalization placement in modern Transformers, but literature reports inconsistent orderings across training regimes. We trace this to an overlooked factor: training curriculum. Standard pre-norm setup assumes end-to-end training, whereas curriculum depth growing introduces blocks incrementally. We hypothesize and verify that Post-norm's self-normalized residual stream provides stable, scale-controlled forward conditioning for newly added blocks during block-stacking depth expansion. In Qwen3-8B distillation experiments, Post-norm reduces validation cross-entropy loss by 0.0328 over Pre-norm and maintains stable residual stream boundary scales.
