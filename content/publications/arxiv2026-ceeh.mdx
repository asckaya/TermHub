Chain-of-Thought (CoT) has substantially empowered Large Language Models (LLMs) to tackle complex reasoning tasks, yet explicit reasoning steps incur prohibitive inference latency and token costs. We identify a critical failure mode: explicitly optimizing for shorter trajectories triggers rapid entropy collapse, which prematurely shrinks the exploration space. To address this, we propose CEEH (Compress the Easy, Explore the Hard), a difficulty-aware approach to RL-based efficient reasoning that dynamically assesses instance difficulty to apply selective entropy regularization. Across six reasoning benchmarks, CEEH consistently reduces response length while maintaining or improving accuracy. Accepted at SIGKDD 2026 (CCF-A Conference, Co-First Author).
